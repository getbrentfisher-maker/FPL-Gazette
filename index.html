⚖️ The Tribunal

Case 1 — The Herd Moves as One. Eight of ten managers handed Erling Haaland the armband — Mancs, Meh United, Jib Jib, Barco Polo, Moist Clams, abc, Puttingonmyjacquet and Byron's Team — and a goal against Sunderland returned 6, doubling to 12 apiece. The Tribunal notes that when eight managers wear the same armband, the verdict is decided everywhere else.

Case 2 — The People vs. The Governor. Dalen Bunce (Haaland'sHaulers2.0) stands accused of the unthinkable: abandoning Haaland the one week Haaland scored. Cole Palmer's 2 points doubled to 4, an 8-point gap to the herd. The defendant offered no comment. The Tribunal considers this a proper captain's mare.

Case 3 — Repeat Offender, Igor Thiago Division (Fifth Count, Acquitted). Andrew Bryce (TBs Turtles) returned to the dock for a fifth straight captaincy of Igor Thiago, and the court must record an unprecedented acquittal: 5 points, doubling to 10, inside a league-best 78. The Tribunal has no precedent for this and reserves judgment on a sixth.

Exhibit A — The Casualty Ward. Submitted into evidence: the post-international-break injury list. Fantasy Football Hub's round-up (updated 7 October) flags Chelsea's João Pedro as a knee doubt, hamstring concerns at Arsenal over Tzolis and Havertz, Bournemouth's Alex Scott facing weeks out and Spurs' Pedro Porro out for several weeks. Haaland, for the record, appears fine — his early exit for Norway looks planned. The deadline bell tolls Saturday at 12:00 SAST; check your doubtful picks before the herd gets to them.
