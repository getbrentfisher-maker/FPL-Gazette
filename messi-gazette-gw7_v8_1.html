⚖️ The Tribunal

Case 1 — The People vs. The Inside Man. Declan Braithwaite stands accused of working for the other side. He had Haaland, who scored twice, and handed the armband to Yoane Wissa: yellow card, 2 points. Haaland got 13. Swap them and Declan is 12 points better off. It’s the second week running on Wissa. Verdict: guilty, and at least he’s consistent.

Case 2 — The Fall Guy, Seventh Count. Andrew Bryce is back in the dock for a seventh straight Thiago armband, worth 8 this time. Lowest score in the league for the second week running (45, then 34), and the wooden spoon. Asked for comment, Andrew said he normally assesses his team around Christmas and checks again in late April. The Tribunal has noted both dates. It is October.

Case 3 — Two Crews, One Safe. Jib Jib and Puttingonmyjacquet both played Triple Captain on Haaland in the same week. The Tribunal checked for collusion and found nothing but a shared lack of imagination. jib lurie needed the chip because he’d just burned a Wildcard. Luke needed it because he’d gone six weeks without one and was feeling left out. Luke’s 89 beat jib lurie’s 84, then he left 24 on the bench, one off the record. Both were outscored by Barco Polo’s 90, which needed no chip at all (see Exhibit A). Verdict: not guilty of conspiracy, guilty of being predictable.

Exhibit A — The Hit Man. Barco Polo, owner of this publication, took a −4 for two transfers and scored 90. Highest in the league, more than either triple-captain, 86 after the hit, and up to 3rd. The Tribunal notes that the Gazette has refused to recuse itself and is calling this a trend. Bench down from 21 to 2 in a week, so he’s finally read the instructions.

Exhibit B — The Fence and the Missing Evidence. Warren Eades sold Bruno Fernandes before GW7, made two more changes, took a −4 and slid from joint 2nd to 4th. Bruno got a yellow at Leeds for 1 point on nobody’s team: not one of the ten squads in this league owned him. After United’s 4–1 loss at Como he reportedly questioned the tactics. The Tribunal notes that Warren questioned them first, on Saturday. Verdict: moved the goods in time, still finished behind Byron once the hit was counted.
